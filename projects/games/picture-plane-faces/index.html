    How the 343 positions work: the triangle is cut into 7 bands along each of its three directions, which makes 49 small triangles (7×7). Each small triangle holds 7 snap points, so there are 7×7×7 = 343 styles. The style ID is those three base-7 digits. Every face is drawn from scratch by code from the three weights; nothing is copied from the book. Based on the "Big Triangle" in Understanding Comics (1993) by Scott McCloud.
    Mode 1 places 49 well-known characters, one per small triangle, in the spirit of McCloud's own chart; the placements are my approximation, not his exact ones. Their pictures load live from Wikipedia and stay the property of their creators.

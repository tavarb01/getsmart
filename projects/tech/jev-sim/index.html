Jev Sim: sorting a million camera frames

Everything here is fake. The "Jev" below is a mock function with made-up accuracy, latency and price. No API, no key, no real photos. It shows the shape of the workflow: cheap judgments, fan-out, thresholds, escalation. Frames are synthetic text descriptions of scenes. The segments describe shopping behavior (groups, dwell time), never who someone is. No faces, no identities.

Audience segments (auto-accepted)

How the pipeline works

frame text ──► Noul  "is a person visible?"        ── no ──► dropped
      │
      └─► (asked together, in parallel)
            Choice "which shopper segment?"  → family | young_adult | senior | commuter | none_of_these
            Noul   "is the person browsing?" → probability
      │
      code: confidence ≥ threshold ? accept : escalate
